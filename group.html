Join Us

I am always searching for motivated graduate students who are interested in

  • Numerical modeling of surface/subsurface flow and reactive transport processes

  • Numerical modeling of surf-zone processes

  • Inverse modeling/data assimilation with uncertainty quantification in earth science

  • Optimization & optimal control of water resources management system

  • Big environmental data analytics with machine learning

Please send an email with your CV and references to lee.group.application@gmail.com

If you are from a non-English speaking country, our program needs your English exam score - TOEFL/IELTS for your application.
